Here are five example questions for each of Amazon's 16 Leadership Principles:

---

### 1. **Customer Obsession**
   1. Describe a time when you went above and beyond for a customer.
   2. How do you prioritize customer needs when resources are limited?
   3. Tell me about a time when a customer had an issue with your product or service. How did you resolve it?
   4. How do you stay in tune with customer needs and feedback?
   5. Describe a situation where you disagreed with a customer. How did you handle it?

---

### 2. **Ownership**
   1. Describe a time when you took ownership of a difficult situation that wasn’t part of your job.
   2. How do you balance taking ownership of your projects while also delegating tasks?
   3. Tell me about a time when you had to make a difficult decision without support.
   4. Have you ever disagreed with company direction? How did you respond?
   5. What would you do if you noticed a recurring issue within your team?

---

### 3. **Invent and Simplify**
   1. Describe an innovative idea you developed to solve a complex problem.
   2. How have you simplified a complicated process or system?
   3. Share a time when your idea wasn’t initially accepted. How did you handle it?
   4. What process improvements have you implemented in previous roles?
   5. Describe a time you were able to identify a simple solution to a complex problem.

---

### 4. **Are Right, A Lot**
   1. Describe a decision you made that wasn’t popular but that you knew was right.
   2. How do you ensure you make the best decisions, even with limited information?
   3. Tell me about a time you were wrong. How did you handle it?
   4. How do you stay informed and make sound decisions in fast-moving situations?
   5. Describe a time when you had to make a judgment call and what was the outcome.

---

### 5. **Learn and Be Curious**
   1. How do you keep up-to-date with industry trends and advancements?
   2. Describe a time when you taught yourself a new skill to complete a project.
   3. What is a recent skill you learned, and how has it benefited your work?
   4. Tell me about a time you pursued a new learning opportunity outside of work.
   5. How do you encourage curiosity and learning in your team?

---

### 6. **Hire and Develop the Best**
   1. Describe a time when you made a hiring decision that you later regretted.
   2. How do you identify top talent and help them grow?
   3. What is your approach to coaching underperforming employees?
   4. Share an example of how you have mentored someone on your team.
   5. Tell me about a time you helped someone achieve their career goals.

---

### 7. **Insist on the Highest Standards**
   1. Describe a time when you raised the bar for your team.
   2. How do you handle situations where your team’s performance falls below expectations?
   3. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a superior about standards.
   4. Describe a project where quality was essential and how you maintained it.
   5. How do you ensure consistency and high standards across projects?

---

### 8. **Think Big**
   1. Share an example of when you took a calculated risk with a large impact.
   2. Describe a time you created a bold vision for your team or project.
   3. How do you balance thinking big with the need for quick wins?
   4. Tell me about a time you identified a new business opportunity and pursued it.
   5. How do you encourage your team to think big and take risks?

---

### 9. **Bias for Action**
   1. Describe a time when you made a quick decision with limited data.
   2. How do you handle decision-making under pressure?
   3. Share an example of when you took a calculated risk that didn’t go as planned.
   4. How do you manage the balance between speed and quality?
   5. Tell me about a time you pushed through a major obstacle to achieve results.

---

### 10. **Frugality**
   1. Describe a time you completed a project with limited resources.
   2. How do you prioritize cost-saving without compromising quality?
   3. Tell me about a time you identified a cost-saving opportunity.
   4. How do you ensure efficiency in your work?
   5. Describe a project where you had to stretch resources to achieve your goals.

---

### 11. **Earn Trust**
   1. Describe a situation where you had to build trust with a skeptical team member.
   2. How do you handle conflicts within your team?
   3. Tell me about a time when you needed to rebuild trust with a client or colleague.
   4. How do you ensure transparency in your work?
   5. Describe a time when you handled confidential information responsibly.

---

### 12. **Dive Deep**
   1. Describe a time you went beyond the standard level of inquiry to solve a problem.
   2. How do you approach complex problems that require extensive analysis?
   3. Tell me about a time when your attention to detail helped avoid a critical error.
   4. How do you balance detailed investigation with the need for timely action?
   5. Share an example of a situation where you had to dig deep to uncover the root cause of an issue.

---

### 13. **Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit**
   1. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a team decision but committed to it.
   2. Describe a situation where you had to push back against leadership.
   3. How do you approach situations where you strongly disagree with others?
   4. Describe a time when you stood up for an unpopular opinion.
   5. How do you ensure respectful debate within your team?

---

### 14. **Deliver Results**
   1. Describe a challenging goal you achieved and how you did it.
   2. Tell me about a time you were under a tight deadline. How did you handle it?
   3. How do you keep your team motivated when faced with high-pressure situations?
   4. Describe a time when you exceeded your own expectations for delivering results.
   5. How do you ensure you deliver results despite obstacles?

---

### 15. **Strive to be Earth’s Best Employer**
   1. Describe an initiative you've led to improve team morale or well-being.
   2. How do you ensure inclusivity and diversity in your team?
   3. Tell me about a time when you made a change to support work-life balance.
   4. How do you foster a positive work environment for everyone?
   5. What steps do you take to ensure your team feels valued and respected?

---

### 16. **Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility**
   1. Describe a time you made a decision that positively impacted the broader community.
   2. How do you consider the broader impact of your work on society or the environment?
   3. Tell me about a time you advocated for ethical practices within your team.
   4. How do you ensure that your team’s work aligns with social responsibility?
   5. Describe a project where you prioritized sustainability over short-term gains.

---
